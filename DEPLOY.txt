Sensum OSRS Dashboard V1.1

Files:
- V1.html                -> replace the existing V1.html in the repo
- DashboardV1.js         -> backend included for completeness; unchanged from V1
- DEPLOY.txt             -> quick deploy instructions

Deploy from a fresh PowerShell:
cd $HOME\Documents\Sensum-OSRS-Dashboard
.\deploy.ps1 -Message "Polish V1.1 progression dashboard"

Then open your existing Apps Script /dev preview URL with ?v=1.

This does NOT redeploy the public /exec deployment.
